# Seller Brief: Philz Coffee Wholesale

## 1. Company name and website

**Company:** Philz Coffee

**Wholesale website:** https://philzcoffee.com/wholesale?utm_source=chatgpt.com

Philz Coffee is a coffee company that serves handcrafted coffee and offers wholesale coffee products and services for business customers.

## 2. Why I chose this company

I chose Philz Coffee because I wanted to gain and demonstrate experience in the retail space. This project will give me a practical example to discuss on my resume and show that I am interested in retail businesses and sales.

I also miss Philz Coffee a lot, so I wanted to create a project that could support a company I personally enjoy and care about.

Philz Coffee meets the business qualifiers for this project because it:

- Has its own public website.
- Provides enough public website information for an agent to build a company profile.
- Sells products or services to other businesses through its wholesale business.
- Has potential business customers with public websites that an agent can research.
- Is a relatively smaller company compared with giant companies such as Amazon or OpenAI.
- Fits a business-to-business (B2B) sales model through wholesale coffee products and services.

## 3. Information to collect for finding appropriate sales targets

The sales agent should collect the following information about potential business customers. These fields will help the agent determine whether an organization is a good fit for Philz Coffee wholesale and prioritize the most promising prospects.

| Field | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Company name | Identifies the prospect and provides a consistent reference across research and outreach. |
| Company website | Gives the agent a reliable public source for researching the prospect and verifying other details. |
| Business type and industry | Helps determine whether the organization is likely to purchase coffee for customers, employees, guests, or events. |
| Location and service area | Helps evaluate geographic fit, delivery feasibility, and whether the prospect is near a Philz Coffee service area. |
| Number of locations | Indicates the potential size of the account and whether it could support a single-location or multi-location opportunity. |
| Workplace or customer traffic | Helps estimate coffee consumption and identify businesses with a meaningful need for a wholesale coffee program. |
| Existing coffee service | Shows whether the prospect already offers coffee, which provider it uses if publicly available, and whether Philz could replace or supplement the current option. |
| Products or services offered | Helps assess whether Philz Coffee products would complement the prospect’s customer experience or employee benefits. |
| Evidence of hospitality or food-and-beverage offerings | Indicates whether coffee is already part of the prospect’s operations and may reveal an immediate sales use case. |
| Target contact and job title | Identifies the likely decision-maker, such as an owner, office manager, hospitality director, food-and-beverage manager, or procurement lead. |
| Contact information | Provides a practical route for outreach while keeping the research focused on publicly available business contact details. |
| Business need or sales use case | Explains why the prospect might buy wholesale coffee, such as office coffee, café service, hotel amenities, catering, or events. |
| Budget or purchasing signals | Helps estimate whether the prospect has the resources and intent to consider a wholesale relationship. |
| Growth, hiring, or expansion signals | May indicate increasing coffee demand or a new location that creates a timely sales opportunity. |
| Brand and customer-experience fit | Helps determine whether Philz’s handcrafted, premium positioning matches the prospect’s brand and customers. |
| Qualification evidence and source links | Makes each recommendation traceable and prevents the agent from inventing facts or treating weak assumptions as evidence. |
| Prospect priority and rationale | Summarizes how attractive the account is and why it should be contacted before other prospects. |
